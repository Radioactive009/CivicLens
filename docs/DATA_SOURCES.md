# CivicLens Data Sources & Licensing

CivicLens operates under the ethical mandate of analyzing **aggregate, publicly accessible open government records**.

## Data Source Standards

1. **Election Commission of India (ECI)**
   - Coverage: General Elections to Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) & Lok Sabha
   - Data Points: Candidate vote counts, party affiliations, winning margins, valid votes, registered electors
   - Licensing: Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India (`https://data.gov.in`)

2. **Directorate of Economics and Statistics, Haryana**
   - Coverage: District statistical handbooks, literacy counts, public health infrastructure
   - Data Points: Literacy rates, workforce participation, primary health centers, rural electrification
   - Licensing: Open Government Data Platform India / State Gazette Archives

3. **Regional Public Issue Monitoring (Demo Mode)**
   - Coverage: Public-interest reporting on infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture, and civic services
   - Tone Analysis: Editorial descriptive sentiment only. Not used to claim direct voter opinion polls.

## Ethical Boundaries
- No individual voter tracking or voter identification lists.
- No psychographic or micro-targeting operations.
- All numbers are aggregations at the constituency, district, or state levels.
